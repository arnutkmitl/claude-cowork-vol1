ANNUAL REPORT SUMMARY: Cedar Peak Software Inc.
Fiscal year: January 1 to December 31, last year

1. Business overview
Cedar Peak sells cloud ERP software to small and mid-sized
businesses. Founded seven years ago; 45 employees; head office
in Denver.

2. Results
Total revenue: $1,845,000 (up 23% on the prior year)
Net profit: $289,000 (15.7% margin)
New customers: 89 companies (target: 80)
Customer retention: 87%
Annual recurring revenue (ARR): $1,791,000

3. Products
- ERP Standard: 420 customers at $250/month; revenue $1,260,000
- ERP Pro: 75 customers at $590/month; revenue $531,000
- Consulting: revenue $54,000

4. Markets and growth
Metro areas: 58% of revenue (up 18%)
Regional markets: 42% (up 31%; the fastest-growing segment)
Industries: retail 35%, manufacturing 28%, services 22%,
other 15%

5. Challenges
- Customer acquisition cost rose 15% as competition increased
- Churn among the smallest customers (1-10 staff) is 18%
- Too few support staff; NPS fell from 42 to 38

6. Plan for this year
- Launch an ERP mobile app (Q2)
- Grow the support team from 5 to 8 people
- Add a partner sales channel
- Revenue target: $2,400,000 (30% growth)
- New customer target: 120 companies
