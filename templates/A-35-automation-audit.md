# A-35: Automation audit

Use once a month to review your automation. It works only if your tasks write a run note or undo log, as A-34 sets up.

```text
Analyze this month's automation logs (the run notes or undo
logs my tasks write).
{ATTACH THE LOGS}

Which tasks ran most and least; the error rate of each; runs
that took unusually long; files that look misfiled; total items
processed. Summarize as a health score from 1 to 10, three
strengths, three improvements, and new tasks worth automating.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Automation.
