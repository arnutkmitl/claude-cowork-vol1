# A-36: Assess a job application

Use to apply the same criteria to every candidate.

> **Warning:** Use this to organize your own review, not to decide. A person must make every hiring decision, and in some places automated screening is regulated, so check your organization's policy and local law before pasting candidates' personal data into Claude.

```text
Assess this application against the criteria.
{PASTE THE APPLICATION}

Role: {TITLE}. Experience: {N}+ years. Core skills: {SKILLS}.
Other requirements: {LIST}. Salary budget: up to {AMOUNT}.

Give a 1-to-5 score per criterion with reasons, three
strengths, two concerns, a category (Shortlist, Review, Not
qualified), and three interview questions.
Judge skills and experience only; ignore age, gender,
nationality, and the prestige of schools. Use only what the
application shows.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, People and hiring.
