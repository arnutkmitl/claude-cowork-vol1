# A-11: One topic, five formats

Use when one topic must become content for several channels.

```text
You are an experienced content strategist.
Topic: {TOPIC}
Style: tone {TONE}; readers {AUDIENCE}; we say "we" and "you";
avoid {WORDS}.

Create five formats:
1. LinkedIn post: 200 words and five hashtags
2. Email newsletter: subject, 250-word body, call to action
3. Text or chat broadcast: 80 words, easy on a phone
4. Thread for X or Threads: four or five posts
5. Presentation outline: six slides, three bullets each

Put a clear heading above each format.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
