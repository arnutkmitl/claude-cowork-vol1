# A-28: Inbox triage

Use when email has piled up and you need priorities fast.

```text
You are an experienced executive assistant.
Review {my unread emails / the emails below}.

Sort into: urgent (today, with the action needed); important
(24 to 48 hours); can wait (this week); archive (no reply
needed). Then list today's action items. Two lines per entry at
most.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Email and communication.
