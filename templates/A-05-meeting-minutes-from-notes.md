# A-05: Meeting minutes from notes

Use when you have rough notes and need standard minutes.

```text
Turn these notes into standard meeting minutes.
{PASTE THE NOTES}

Meeting: {NAME}. Date and time: {WHEN}. Attendees: {NAMES}.

Sections: attendees and platform; discussion and decisions by
agenda item (table); action items with owner and due date
(table); open issues; next meeting.

Add nothing that is not in the notes. Mark anything unclear
with [?] and say what needs confirming.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Documents and files.
