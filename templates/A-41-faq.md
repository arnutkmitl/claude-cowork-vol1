# A-41: FAQ

Use to answer the common questions about a product, service, or topic.

```text
Write an FAQ for {PRODUCT, SERVICE, OR TOPIC}.
Source information: {PASTE OR ATTACH}
Who asks: {AUDIENCE}. Where it will appear: {CHANNEL}.

The 10 most common questions, each with a two- or
three-sentence answer, from basic to specific. Use customers' words,
not jargon.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Learning and training.
