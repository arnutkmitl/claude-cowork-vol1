# A-16: Business data analysis

Use when you have a dataset and need to know what it means for the business.

```text
You are an experienced business analyst.
{ATTACH THE FILE OR PASTE THE DATA}

Business: {TYPE}. Period: {PERIOD}. Purpose: {PURPOSE}.
Readers: {WHO}.

Cover: overview and main trends; strengths; concerns; three
actions to take now, by priority. Use only figures from the
data; never invent numbers.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Data analysis.
