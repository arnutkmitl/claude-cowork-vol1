# A-44: Recommendation with SCQA

Use to present a recommendation persuasively.

```text
Present a recommendation on {TOPIC} using SCQA.
Information: {PASTE OR DESCRIBE}

Situation: where we are. Complication: what has changed or gone
wrong. Question: what we must decide. Answer: the best
recommendation.
Then an action plan: 30-day quick wins, three-to-six-month
actions, and long-term investments.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Thinking tools and routines.
