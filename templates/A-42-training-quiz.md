# A-42: Training quiz

Use to check learning after a training session.

```text
Create a quiz on {TOPIC} for {AUDIENCE AND LEVEL}.
Source material: {PASTE OR ATTACH}

{N} multiple-choice questions (four options), {N} true-or-false
questions with explanations, {N} short-answer questions, and
{N} scenario questions. For each: the answer, a short
explanation, and a difficulty level.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Learning and training.
