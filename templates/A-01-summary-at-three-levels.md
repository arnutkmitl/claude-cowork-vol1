# A-01: Summary at three levels

Use when a long document must serve readers with different amounts of time.

```text
{ATTACH THE FILE OR PASTE THE CONTENT}

Summarize this document at three levels:
Level 1: one sentence (under 50 words), for team chat.
Level 2: five bullets, no more than two sentences each, for a
team briefing.
Level 3: an executive summary of 150 to 200 words, with the
main point, key figures, and what must happen next.

Do not add anything that is not in the document.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Documents and files.
