# A-09: Bad news or a problem

Use when you must report a delay, a problem, or an incident.

```text
Draft an email reporting a problem.
Me: {ROLE}. To: {client / management / team}.
What happened: {DESCRIPTION}
Impact: {WHAT, WHO, HOW MUCH}
What we are doing: {ACTIONS}
Expected resolution: {WHEN}
What I need from them: {ACTION OR AWARENESS}
Tone: accountable and direct; no excuses; clear next steps.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
