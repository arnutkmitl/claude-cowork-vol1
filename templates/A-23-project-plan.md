# A-23: Project plan

Use when starting a new project.

```text
Think like an experienced project manager.
Project: {NAME AND GOAL}
Resources: {N} people, budget {AMOUNT}, {N} months
Known challenges: {CONSTRAINTS}

Plan: scope (in and out); five milestones with dates; top three
risks with responses; who does what in each phase; how success
will be measured. Keep it realistic for these resources.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Planning and decisions.
