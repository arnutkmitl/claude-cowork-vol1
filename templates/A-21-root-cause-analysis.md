# A-21: Root-cause analysis

Use when you need the real cause of a problem before fixing it.

```text
Analyze this problem.
Problem: {WHAT AND ITS IMPACT}. Background: {CONTEXT}.
Already tried: {WHAT AND RESULT}. Constraints: {LIMITS}.

1. Symptoms versus causes
2. The three most likely root causes (five whys)
3. A quick fix, a medium-term fix, and a long-term fix
4. Where to start, and why
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Planning and decisions.
