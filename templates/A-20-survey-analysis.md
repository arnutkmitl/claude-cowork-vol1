# A-20: Survey analysis

Use when you have survey results and need to know what they say.

```text
Analyze the results of {SURVEY NAME}.
Respondents: {N}. Questions and responses: {DATA}

1. Net Promoter Score, if there is a 0-to-10 question:
   calculate and explain it
2. Themes in open-ended answers, with counts
3. Differences between segments, if the data has them
4. A priority matrix: importance against current score
5. Three to five representative quotes, positive and negative
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Data analysis.
