# A-04: Compare vendor proposals

Use when you must choose a vendor or contractor from several proposals.

```text
{ATTACH ALL PROPOSALS}

Compare these {N} proposals in a table:
- Price: setup and ongoing
- Scope of work
- Delivery timeline
- Warranty and support
- Special terms

Then: which offers the best value, what each proposal is
missing, which you recommend and why, and three questions to
ask before deciding.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Documents and files.
