# A-48: Weekly report

Use to produce the same weekly report, in the same format, in minutes.

```text
Create the weekly report for week {N}.
This week: revenue {X} (target {Y}); new orders {N}; new
customers {N}; support tickets {N} ({N} resolved); {OTHER KPIS}.
Last week: {DATA}. Monthly target: {DATA}.

Sections: key figures (table with % of target and change from
last week); three things that went well; two things to watch;
three priorities for next week. 300 to 400 words.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Thinking tools and routines.
