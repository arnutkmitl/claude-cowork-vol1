# A-13: Copyedit

Use when the text should read more smoothly without changing its meaning.

```text
Copyedit this text.
{PASTE THE TEXT}

Make it easier to read, cut repetition and padding, keep the
register consistent ({neutral / formal}), and keep the meaning
and tone. Return the new version and a bulleted summary of the
changes.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
