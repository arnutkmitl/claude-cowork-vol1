# A-38: Interview questions

Use to prepare a structured interview.

```text
Create interview questions for {ROLE}.
Skills to assess: {1}, {2}, {3}.

Five behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time when..."),
three situational questions ("What would you do if..."), and
three technical or role-specific questions.
For each, list what a strong answer would include.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, People and hiring.
