# A-12: Proofread

Use when you want spelling, grammar, and punctuation fixed, and nothing else.

```text
Proofread this text.
{PASTE THE TEXT}

Fix spelling, grammar, and punctuation. Do not change the
content, wording, or sentence structure. Return the corrected
text, then a list of the changes with brief reasons.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
