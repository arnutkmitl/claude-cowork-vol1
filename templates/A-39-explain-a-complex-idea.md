# A-39: Explain a complex idea

Use to understand a difficult topic, or to explain one.

```text
Explain {TOPIC} to {AUDIENCE, e.g., a manager with no IT
background}.
Use everyday analogies; avoid jargon or explain it when used.
Structure: what it is, how it works, why it matters, a real
example. {N} paragraphs; understandable in three minutes.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Learning and training.
