# A-14: Translate and localize

Use when content must read naturally for readers in another market.

```text
Translate and localize this text from {SOURCE LANGUAGE} into
{TARGET LANGUAGE} for readers in {COUNTRY OR REGION}.
---
{PASTE THE TEXT}
---
Natural phrasing, not word for word. Register: {LEVEL}. Adapt
examples for these readers. Convert currency to {CURRENCY} at
{RATE}, and dates and units to local conventions. Keep terms
such as AI, cloud, and API in English.
Return the translation and notes on choices I should check.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
