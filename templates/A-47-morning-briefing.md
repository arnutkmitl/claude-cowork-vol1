# A-47: Morning briefing

Use every morning to see the day at a glance.

```text
Morning briefing for {DATE}.
Email since yesterday evening: {my inbox, or PASTE}
Today's calendar: {my calendar, or PASTE}

Layout: new and urgent email counts; number of events and the
first one; two things to do first; today's top three priorities;
reminders. Under 150 words; readable in 30 seconds.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Thinking tools and routines.
