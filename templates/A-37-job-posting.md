# A-37: Job posting

Use when you open a new position.

```text
Write a job posting for {ROLE}.
Company: {NAME}, {WHAT IT DOES}. Culture: {TWO OR THREE POINTS}.
Responsibilities: {THREE TO FIVE}. Requirements: {LIST}.
Nice to have: {LIST}. Salary range: {IF DISCLOSED}.
Benefits: {LIST}.
Tone: {friendly / formal}, reflecting our culture. Under 350
words, with a clear call to apply. Use inclusive language.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, People and hiring.
