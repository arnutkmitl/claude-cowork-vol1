# A-19: Three scenarios

Use when planning under uncertainty.

```text
Using this data on {TOPIC}:
{DATA}

Build three scenarios:
Base case: current trends continue. Forecast {METRIC}, with
reasons from the data.
Optimistic case: assume {ASSUMPTION}. Forecast, and what must
happen to achieve it.
Pessimistic case: assume {ASSUMPTION}. Forecast, and a
contingency plan of two or three steps.

End with a table comparing the three.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Data analysis.
