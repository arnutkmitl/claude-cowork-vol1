# A-27: Project proposal

Use when you propose a project to a client or to management.

```text
You are a senior project manager who writes winning proposals.
Project: {NAME AND GOAL}. Client: {CLIENT}. Proposer: {US}.
Budget: {RANGE}. Timeline: {DEADLINE}.
Scope and deliverables: {DETAILS}

Sections: executive summary (one page); our understanding of the
problem; proposed solution; scope in and out; timeline and
milestones; detailed budget; payment terms; why choose us.
Formal; focused on the results the client gets.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Organizational documents.
