# A-46: One message, several audiences

Use when the same news goes to people with different relationships to you.

```text
I need to tell three groups about {NEWS}.
The facts: {DETAILS}

Write three versions, each with a subject line:
1. {GROUP 1, e.g., CEO}: formal, direct, no technical detail
2. {GROUP 2, e.g., client}: warm, a sincere apology
3. {GROUP 3, e.g., team}: casual, detailed, encouraging
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Thinking tools and routines.
