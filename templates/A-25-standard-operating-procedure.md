# A-25: Standard operating procedure

Use when a process needs a written procedure.

```text
Write an SOP for this process.
Process: {NAME}. Owner: {DEPARTMENT}. Frequency: {HOW OFTEN}.
People involved: {ROLES}. Known steps: {LIST}. Tools: {TOOLS}.

Include: header (SOP number, version, date, approver); purpose
and scope; numbered steps with the person responsible; a text
flowchart with arrows; success criteria; revision history.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Organizational documents.
