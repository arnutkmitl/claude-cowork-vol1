# A-17: Sales analysis

Use when you need a closer look at sales performance.

```text
Analyze this sales data.
{DATA}

1. Top three and bottom three products or services
2. Strongest and weakest periods
3. Worrying or interesting trends
4. To grow sales {N}% next quarter, where to focus first

Give a three-sentence executive summary, the detail, and a
table of key figures.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Data analysis.
