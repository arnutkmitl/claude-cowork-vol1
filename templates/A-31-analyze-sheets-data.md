# A-31: Analyze Sheets data

Use with data from Google Sheets.

```text
Here is data from the Google Sheet "{NAME}":
{ATTACH, PASTE, or: open it from my Drive}

Answer: {QUESTION 1}; {QUESTION 2}; {QUESTION 3}.
Also tell me what data looks missing or abnormal, and what
extra data would allow a deeper analysis. Use tables and short
explanations. Use only figures from the data.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Google Workspace.
