# A-15: Pre-send check

Use before an important document goes out.

```text
Check this {email / report / proposal} before I send it.
{PASTE THE TEXT}
Recipient: {WHO}. Purpose: {WHAT THEY SHOULD DO OR FEEL}.

Assess clarity, tone, completeness, call to action, and risk
of misreading. Give a score out of 10 and specific suggestions.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
