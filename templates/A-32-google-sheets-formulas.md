# A-32: Google Sheets formulas

Use when you know what to calculate but not how.

```text
Write Google Sheets formulas.
My sheet: column A: {CONTENT AND TYPE}; column B: {...};
column C: {...}.
I need: 1. {CALCULATION IN PLAIN WORDS} 2. {...} 3. {...}

For each: the formula, a one- or two-sentence explanation, and
the best cell for it.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Google Workspace.
