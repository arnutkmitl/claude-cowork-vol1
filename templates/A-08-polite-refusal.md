# A-08: Polite refusal

Use when you must decline a request, offer, or invitation and keep the relationship.

```text
Draft an email declining this request.
Me: {ROLE}. To: {NAME, RELATIONSHIP}.
The request: {WHAT THEY ASKED}
The real reason (context only): {REASON}
What I can offer instead: {e.g., a later date, a referral}
Tone: warm and respectful. Under 120 words.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
