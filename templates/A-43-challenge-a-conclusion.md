# A-43: Challenge a conclusion

Use before you act on an important conclusion.

```text
You concluded: {CONCLUSION}.
Now argue against it as hard as you can. How else could the
data be explained? Which assumptions might be wrong? When would
this conclusion fail?
Then say under which conditions the original conclusion still
holds, and what to watch for when applying it.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Thinking tools and routines.
