# A-30: Long thread summary

Use before replying to a long email thread.

```text
Summarize this email thread.
{PASTE THE THREAD, or: the thread with {NAME} about {SUBJECT}}

Background; current status and who is waiting for whom; open
issues; what is expected of me. Under 150 words.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Email and communication.
