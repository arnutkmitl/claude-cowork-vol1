# A-06: Business email

Use for any professional email.

```text
Draft an email.
From: {NAME}, {ROLE} at {COMPANY}
To: {NAME}, {ROLE OR RELATIONSHIP}
Purpose: {WHAT YOU NEED TO COMMUNICATE}
Must include: {KEY FACTS}
Tone: {formal / neutral / friendly}
Length: under {N} words

Give a formal and a friendly version, each with a subject line,
and end with one or two things to check before sending.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
