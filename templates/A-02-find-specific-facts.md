# A-02: Find specific facts

Use when you need particular facts from a long document without reading all of it.

```text
{ATTACH THE FILE OR PASTE THE CONTENT}

Find and list:
1. {e.g., every figure related to the budget}
2. {e.g., every date and deadline}
3. {e.g., every person named, with their role}

For each item, give the section or paragraph and quote the
relevant sentence (two sentences at most). If an item is not in
the document, write "Not found in the document."
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Documents and files.
