# A-03: Compare two versions

Use when you need to know what changed in a revised document.

```text
Compare two versions of this document.
Version A (older): {PASTE OR ATTACH}
Version B (newer): {PASTE OR ATTACH}

1. What B adds
2. What B removes
3. What both contain but say differently
4. Whether B is better, and what still needs work

Show the differences in a table, with a short commentary.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Documents and files.
