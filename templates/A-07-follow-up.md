# A-07: Follow-up

Use when you have sent something and heard nothing back.

```text
Draft a follow-up email.
Me: {NAME, ROLE}. To: {NAME, ROLE}.
Following up on: {e.g., a quote, a contract, a task}
First sent: {DATE}. Days without a reply: {N}.
What I need: {e.g., a reply by a date, confirmation, a meeting}
Tone: polite, not pushy, but clear that it matters.
Under 100 words.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
