# A-22: Compare options

Use when choosing between several options.

```text
Compare {WHAT} for {PURPOSE}.
Options: A: {DETAILS}. B: {DETAILS}. C: {DETAILS, IF ANY}.
My criteria, most important first: {1}, {2}, {3}.

Give a comparison table scored 1 to 5, your recommendation with
reasons, when each option would be best, and the main risk of
the option you recommend.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Planning and decisions.
