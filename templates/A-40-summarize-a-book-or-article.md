# A-40: Summarize a book or article

Use after reading something worth keeping.

```text
{PASTE OR ATTACH THE TEXT}

Summarize: the main argument (one sentence); five key ideas
with short explanations; the most interesting evidence; three
takeaways I can act on; counterarguments or limitations.
Under 300 words.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Learning and training.
