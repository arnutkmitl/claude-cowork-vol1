# A-10: Blog post or article

Use when you write for a website or blog.

```text
Write a blog post titled "{TITLE}".
Structure: an opening about the reader's problem ({N} words);
{N} main points, each with a heading and two or three sentences;
a closing call to action ({N} words).
Style: {neutral / friendly}, easy to read, with practical
examples. Length: {N} words.
Primary search keyword: {KEYWORD}. Readers: {WHO}.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Writing and content.
