# A-24: Specific recommendations

Use when recommendations must be actionable, not vague.

```text
Based on this data and analysis, give three to five
recommendations, most important first, mixing quick wins and
longer-term initiatives. For each: what, why (with figures),
how (two or three steps), when, and expected impact.
Avoid vague advice such as "increase marketing"; say exactly
what, where, and by when.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Planning and decisions.
