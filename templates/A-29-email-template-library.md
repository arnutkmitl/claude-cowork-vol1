# A-29: Email template library

Use to create a full set of templates for a team.

```text
Create an email template library for {ROLE OR TEAM}.
Style: tone {TONE}; we say "we" and address customers as "you".

Templates for: first outreach; sending a proposal; follow-up
after three days; follow-up after a week; meeting request;
post-meeting summary; confirming a deal; check-in 30 days
after the sale.

For each: a name, a subject line, the body with {PLACEHOLDERS},
and a list of the placeholders.
```

Source: *Claude Cowork: Build Your Own AI Team*, Appendix A, Email and communication.
