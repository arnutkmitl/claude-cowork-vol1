## Walkthrough: test your connected workspace

Use a new conversation and send them one at a time.

### Test 1: inbox summary

```text
Summarize the 10 latest emails in my inbox and tell me which ones
need a reply today.
```

### Test 2: draft reply

```text
Find the latest email from {NAME} and draft a short reply,
neutral and professional, under 80 words. Do not send it.
```

### Test 3: search

```text
Search my email for messages that mention "{KEYWORD}". How many
are there, and what are they about?
```

### Test 4: calendar

```text
Summarize my week. Which day is the most overloaded?
```

### Test 5: draft a meeting

```text
Draft a 30-minute team meeting on Wednesday afternoon in a free
slot. Subject: Q1 review. Show me the details before you create it.
```
