# Style guide: BrightPath Solutions

## Voice
- Friendly and warm, but always professional
- Focused on long-term relationships with customers
- Refers to itself as "we" or "our team," rarely by name

## Addressing readers
- The organization: "we," "our team"
- The reader: "you," or by name in one-to-one messages

## Words we use
grow, trust, support, solution, partner

## Words we avoid
cheap, expensive, "limited time," hard-sell phrases
